# Planet and Moon maps

Source: Solar System Scope — https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Download URLs: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_NAME.jpg
Names: mercury, venus_surface (research comparison), venus_atmosphere (displayed), earth_daymap (saved as earth.jpg), moon, mars, jupiter, saturn, uranus, neptune.

Distributed by Solar System Scope under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The provider describes these as NASA-data-based maps, with adjusted colors and fictional infill where mapping is incomplete. Earth is based on Blue Marble/geodata; these are not all raw unmodified NASA imagery.

Modifications here: runtime resampling and conversion to monochrome luminance, stippled display, directional shading. Original downloaded JPEG files remain unchanged.
